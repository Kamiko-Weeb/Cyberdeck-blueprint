Project: Rugged Off-Grid Portable Computer (“Cyberdeck”)
Purpose: Offline access to Wikipedia, first aid guides, survival manuals, and maps while hiking and camping. No internet required. Must survive field use in a rugged waterproof case.
Deadline: Live demo April 20, 2027. Grade 12 capstone project.

Core Computer
	•	Raspberry Pi 5 8GB
	•	Raspberry Pi 5 Active Cooler (required, non-negotiable in enclosed case)
	•	128GB A2 microSD for OS only
	•	Crucial X9 1TB USB 3.2 external SSD for all data storage (Wikipedia, maps, survival guides)
Display
	•	Elecrow 10.1 inch IPS 1280x800 touchscreen
	•	Connects via HDMI for video and USB for touch input
	•	Intended to mount in the lid of the Pelican case
Input
	•	Logitech K400 Plus wireless keyboard with built-in trackpad
	•	Sits in the base of the case when closed
Case
	•	Pelican 1500 Protector Case
	•	Interior dimensions: 16.75” x 11.18” x 6.12” deep
	•	Fully sealed and waterproof
	•	Internal layout split into three zones: battery left, Pi and cooler center, power electronics right
	•	Screen mounts in lid
	•	All internal mounts and brackets will be 3D printed and designed in Fusion 360 before any printing begins
Power System
	•	Venom VEN15187 LiPo battery, 11.1V 3S, 10500mAh, 50C, EC5 connector
	•	Estimated runtime approximately 6-7 hours at 15W average draw
	•	EC5 to XT60 adapter to interface battery with power system
	•	DROK step-down buck converter, 9-36V input, 5V 5A output, converts battery voltage to stable 5V for Pi
	•	Circuit order: battery → toggle switch → inline fuse (5-7.5A ATC blade fuse) → buck converter → Pi
	•	18 AWG silicone stranded wire for all power lines
	•	22 AWG silicone stranded wire for GPIO and signal lines only
	•	Heat shrink tubing on every soldered joint
	•	Panel mount toggle switch on exterior of case for master power on/off
	•	LiPo charged using iMAX B6 balance charger, opening case to access battery directly for now
	•	LiPo stored in fireproof LiPo safe bag
USB and Connectivity
	•	UGREEN 7-port powered USB hub, 5 USB-A and 2 USB-C ports
	•	Handles keyboard dongle, SSD, touchscreen touch input, and any additional peripherals
Mounting Hardware
	•	LuKaiSen 860 piece M2-M5 screw, nut, and washer kit for general brackets and structural mounts
	•	Separate M2.5 specific kit for Raspberry Pi board mounting (Pi uses M2.5 mounting holes)
	•	LuKaiSen 180 piece brass heat set insert kit covering M2 through M5 for all 3D printed bracket connections
	•	All brackets designed in Fusion 360 before printing
Cooling
	•	Active cooler on Pi
	•	Case requires intake and exhaust vents, exact placement to be determined in Fusion 360 layout
	•	Pi positioned in center zone with airflow clearance on both sides
Optional / Deferred
	•	ESP32 Marauder via USB only, not permanently integrated in version 1
	•	XT60 panel mount external charging port, deferred until external charging proves necessary
Software Plan
	•	Offline Wikipedia dump
	•	Offline maps
	•	First aid and survival guides
	•	RAG-based local AI search assistant for quickly finding relevant information in stored documents during emergencies, architecture to be designed in September 2026
Total Cost : Approximately $1,200 CAD
